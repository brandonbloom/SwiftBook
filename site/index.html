Your previews are already tests.

SwiftBook snapshot-tests every #Preview in light and dark, on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and diffs them against references in git. No stories to write, no service to sign up for.

Get started Read the docs

Stories are your previews

Every #Preview is a story. Nothing to register, wrap or configure. If it works in Xcode's canvas, it works in SwiftBook.

The same stories are your tests

One macro line runs every story under swift test. Perceptual diffs, readable failures, and a review page for every run.

One app to leave open

A Mac viewer shows every story and redraws as your code compiles. Crashes, hangs and build errors never take it down.

One file of setup

import SwiftBookTesting
import Testing

#SwiftBookTests

Then run swift test. The full guide takes five minutes.

SwiftBook is early and private. Interfaces may change, and there are no tagged releases yet.
